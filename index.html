How a timed slot works

  1. Choose your experience. Filter by state, terrain, price or women instructors.
  2. Pick an arrival window. Each window has a fixed number of spots, so we only sell what the operator can handle.
  3. Walk in any time inside it. Show your booking code and start. No counter, no queue.

  • Insurance cover at ₹29 per person, shown clearly at checkout
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before your slot
